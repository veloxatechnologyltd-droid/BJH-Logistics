## Scope

**In:** campaigns (target service, industry, location, keywords, exclusions, fit rules) → business search → duplicate check (within the campaign **and** against existing customers and leads) → website contact enrichment → AI fit score with a reason → staff review → "Add to leads" → approved AI email outreach, staff-composed mail and campaign/address broadcasts → full sync of the sales mailbox's sent and received email (including messages sent outside the platform) → replies and follow-ups → existing quote/job flow.

**Out (SaaS-only in Venariq):** multi-tenancy, billing and usage plans, onboarding, Gmail and Microsoft sign-in, Calendly. **Later:** Hunter.io, WhatsApp/SMS outreach.

## Design rules

- Staff only. Customers never see campaigns, prospects, scores or outreach.
- **Prospects are not leads.** Search results go to `app.prospect`; staff promote chosen ones to `app.lead` (`source='campaign'`, `campaign_id`). The Leads board only shows real leads.
- **Two separate pipelines, never mixed (Evans, 2026-11-12; built 2026-11-12, local only).** A lead found by a campaign is _generated_; every other lead is _manual_. The pipeline comes from the lead's existing `source` (`campaign` = generated), which is set once: a lead cannot be logged by hand as `campaign` and the source cannot be edited, so no separate `origin` column was added. Customers > Leads and `GET /leads?pipeline=manual` show manual leads only; Sales > Pipeline and `?pipeline=generated` show generated leads only. Generated leads have two extra stages, **Replied** and **Interested** (New, Contacted, Replied, Interested, Quoted, Won, Lost); manual keeps New, Contacted, Quoted, Won, Lost. The API and a database constraint refuse Replied/Interested on a manual lead. The Sales overview shows both pipelines side by side (leads, open, won, lost, won of decided, days to win), never added together. Stage names are Evans's open decision D-L5.
- AI-generated outreach must be reviewed and approved by a person before sending. A staff member may send a message they wrote themselves, including a broadcast, by explicitly pressing Send. No automatic sending.
- The email workspace is staff-only, using the Sales hub roles (sales rep, customer service rep and super admin). It syncs the whole sales mailbox, including sent mail sent outside the platform. Broadcast recipients may come from selected campaigns, individually selected email addresses, or both.
- Mail status must distinguish provider acceptance from confirmed delivery; SMTP acceptance alone is not proof of delivery. Opens and clicks are not included in the agreed tracking scope.
- Background work follows the as-built pattern: PostgreSQL queue tables with claim columns, a timer inside the API process, no Redis. Long searches advance one step per tick.
- External providers sit behind interfaces with a **stub** for local runs and tests; no real Apify, AI or SMTP calls in tests.
- Schema only through reviewed `supabase/migrations/`, applied locally only until a release is requested.

## Venariq gaps fixed rather than copied

- An unsubscribe reply writes a suppression entry, not just a lead status.
- Daily send counter is a single atomic `UPDATE`.
- Booking-link auto-send removed; everything needs approval.
- Reply check only scans conversations awaiting a reply (no N+1 over all conversations).
